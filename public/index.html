Full on-screen multi-touch controls are provided automatically. Use left thumb for steering arrows, right thumb for GAS/BRAKE pedals, and tap dedicated action buttons for NITRO, DRIFT, and HORN.

🏆 GAMEPLAY OBJECTIVES

  • Open-World Free Roam: Cruise across 4 massive NCR regions (Gurugram Cyber City, Delhi Central Vista, Yamuna River Bridge, and Noida Expressway).
  • Street Racing Events: Discover sprint and circuit races marked on your GPS radar to earn high Cash (₹) and Reputation (REP).
  • Speed Trap Cameras: Blast through expressway radar cameras above target speeds for instant cash bonuses while watching your In-Car Radar Detector.
  • Evade Police Pursuits: Break speed limits to trigger PCR police cruiser pursuits. Outrun the interceptors to escape bust penalties!
  • Garage Upgrades: Unlock 5 vehicles, 8 custom gloss paints, 8 underglow ground neon kits, and stage-3 performance engine upgrades.
